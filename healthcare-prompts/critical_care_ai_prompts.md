# Critical Care AI Prompt Collection

## Purpose

This collection contains prompt templates designed for critical care, patient safety, clinical reasoning, and healthcare communication tasks. The prompts can be used for AI evaluation, prompt engineering experiments, and healthcare-focused model assessments.

---

## Clinical Recognition and Escalation

### Scenario

Act as an experienced intensive care nurse.

Describe the early indicators of septic shock in an adult patient and explain why timely recognition and intervention are essential.

### Evaluation Focus

* Clinical accuracy
* Safety awareness
* Communication clarity

Analyze the ICU patient report below and produce a concise summary.

* Retain critical clinical findings

## Patient Education Task

### Scenario

Explain mechanical ventilation to a patient or family member using clear, non-technical language.

### Requirements

* Avoid medical jargon
* Explain the purpose of ventilation
* Describe what patients can expect
* Address common concerns

### Evaluation Focus

* Readability
* Patient-centered communication
* Clarity
